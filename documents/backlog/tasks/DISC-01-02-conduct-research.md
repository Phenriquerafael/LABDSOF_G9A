## Objective and scope

Collect evidence of real coordination problems and constraints, with the whole team sharing sessions where practical.

Existing drafts are inputs to review and improve; do not recreate work already supported by evidence. Preparation may overlap predecessor work; confirm dependent conclusions only after the needed findings/decisions exist.

Excluded: Claiming representative results from a convenience sample or treating stated adoption intent as a purchase commitment.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Record actual methods, dates, coded participants/sources and researchers; distinguish professional administrators, volunteers, residents and contractors.
- [ ] Capture concrete past incidents, coordination effort, tools, approvals and scheduling practices; distinguish estimates from measurements.
- [ ] Store restricted notes according to the agreed plan and publish only suitable coded summaries/source references.
- [ ] If recruitment fails or coverage is limited, record attempts, actual limitations and alternative public evidence; do not report interviews or validation that did not occur.
